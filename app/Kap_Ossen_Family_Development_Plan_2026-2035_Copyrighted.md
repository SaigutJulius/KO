## 1. Project Overview

The Kap Ossen Family Development Plan is a long-term family strategy designed to strengthen unity, preserve family heritage and create sustainable opportunities for present and future generations.

The plan brings together education, agriculture, technology, entrepreneurship, mentorship, leadership, international opportunities and mutual family support within one coordinated development framework.

It recognizes that every family member has different abilities, experiences and circumstances. Therefore, contribution is not limited to money. Members can contribute through time, knowledge, mentorship, professional skills, encouragement, networks, resources and service.


## 2. Our Shared Vision

To become a united, educated, innovative and economically empowered family that creates opportunities for present and future generations locally and internationally while contributing positively to Kenya and the world.


## 3. Our Mission

To transform family connection into practical opportunity through education, mentorship, technology, agriculture, entrepreneurship, responsible leadership and mutual support.

## 5. Strategic Objectives

The plan aims to:

- Strengthen relationships and cooperation among family members.
- Document and protect the family’s history and heritage.
- Improve access to education, skills and career opportunities.
- Create structured mentorship between generations.
- Support modern agriculture and agricultural value addition.
- Develop responsible businesses and digital enterprises.
- Connect family members in Kenya and the diaspora.
- Prepare members for lawful international study and employment.
- Promote transparent governance and responsible decision-making.
- Establish sustainable projects that can benefit future generations.

## 8. Scof Official Price And 2029 Target

CURRENT OFFICIAL SCOF PRICE

1 SCOF = KSh 165

This is the official issuer-set SCOF price and the baseline for the development strategy.


SCOF PERFORMANCE TARGET

1 SCOF = KSh 565

Target date: 31 October 2029


TARGET VALUE MOVEMENT

Current official price: KSh 165

Target price: KSh 565

Required movement: +KSh 400 per SCOF

Target multiple: approximately 3.42 times the current official price

Required percentage uplift: approximately 242.4%


9. PURPOSE OF THE KSh 565 TARGET

The KSh 565 target should represent measurable development within the SCOF ecosystem.

The target should be supported by:

- Working agricultural products and services
- Verified ecosystem utility
- Active farmers, buyers and partners
- Repeat marketplace activity
- Sustainable ecosystem revenue
- Transparent supply and treasury policies
- Responsible price governance
- Accurate reporting
- Independent verification where appropriate
- Trust from participants and partners

The value should be connected to verified performance rather than unsupported publicity or speculation.

Measure the number and quality of working products and services within the SCOF ecosystem.


ADOPTION

Measure verified farmers, buyers, partners and other active participants.


COMMERCE

Measure transactions, repeat marketplace activity and agricultural trade supported by the ecosystem.


REVENUE

Measure sustainable income generated through legitimate ecosystem activities.


TRUST

Measure transparency, reporting quality, governance compliance, audits and participant confidence.


## 12. Important Target Discipline

KSh 565 is the SCOF performance target for 31 October 2029.

It is not the current price and should not be described as guaranteed future value. Progress toward the target must be supported by verified ecosystem utility, adoption, commercial activity, revenue, transparent governance and responsible reporting.

Any public communication should distinguish clearly between:

- The current official issuer-set price of KSh 165
- The future performance target of KSh 565
- Verified ecosystem results
- Projections and future expectations

## 15. Safeguards And Responsible Governance

VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

Participation in family initiatives must be voluntary. No member should be pressured or coerced.


TRANSPARENT DECISIONS

Every approved project should have clear owners, objectives, records, budgets and accountability procedures.


LAWFUL OPPORTUNITIES

Migration, employment, education, fundraising, markets and digital activities must follow applicable laws and regulations.


EDUCATION BEFORE RISK

Financial markets and digital assets should be approached through education, careful risk assessment and responsible governance.


DATA PROTECTION

Private family information should not be published without permission. Sensitive family records should be protected through appropriate access controls.


REGULAR REVIEW

Family and SCOF projects should be reviewed regularly to determine what is working, what requires improvement and what should be discontinued.
