# ST-Firm Copyright and Document-Control Standard

**Standard ID:** ST-FIRM-IP-STD-2026-01  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective period:** 2026–2027  
**Owner:** ST-Firm  
**Status:** Internal corporate standard

### Kap Ossen Family Heritage works

> **© 2026–2027 ST-Firm | Kap Ossen Family Heritage Project. All Rights Reserved.**  
> **ST-Firm — Idee Meet’s Tech.**

## 2. Mandatory Placement

The notice must appear on:

- The cover or first page of formal documents.
- The footer or final page of reports, PDFs and presentations.
- The README and licence files of repositories.
- The footer of websites and family portals.
- Metadata or source-code headers where technically appropriate.
- Image captions or watermarks for ST-Firm-owned designs, where practical.

## 3. Covered Original Material

The notice may cover original ST-Firm-created:

- Writing, editing, structure and compilation.
- Research organisation and analytical presentation.
- Designs, diagrams, layouts and visual systems.
- Software, source code, documentation and database structures.
- Family-history narratives written and arranged by ST-Firm.
- Development plans, governance frameworks and operating models.

## 4. Material Not Automatically Claimed

ST-Firm does not claim exclusive ownership merely by including:

- Historical facts or public-domain information.
- Government records or third-party publications.
- Family members’ identities, life facts or personal data.
- Photographs, letters, recordings or documents owned by relatives or third parties.
- External logos, trademarks, quotations or licensed material.

Those items require correct attribution, permission and separate rights management.

## 5. Confidentiality Labels

Use one of the following:

- **Public**
- **Internal**
- **Private Family Working Document**
- **Confidential — Controlled Access**
- **Restricted — Executive/Legal Access Only**

Sensitive oral histories, land records, wills, addresses, allegations, political-pressure accounts and personal documents must default to **Confidential — Controlled Access** unless specifically approved for release.

## 6. Version-Control Format

Recommended footer:

```text
Document ID: [PROJECT]-[TYPE]-[YEAR]-[NUMBER]
Version: [X.Y]
Classification: [LEVEL]
© 2026–2027 ST-Firm. All Rights Reserved.
ST-Firm — Idee Meet’s Tech.
```

## 7. Approval Rule

Publication requires approval from the responsible document owner and, for sensitive family material, the affected family branch or authorised family governance body.
