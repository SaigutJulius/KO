## 2. Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning | Permitted wording |
|---|---|---|
| A — official | Gazette, court, regulator, official institution or authenticated primary record | State the fact precisely, after confirming it concerns the correct person |
| B — independently reported | Credible reporting or a reliable third-party record with identifiable sourcing | Attribute the fact and preserve the source |
| C — self-published | Candidate website, personal profile, company page or campaign material | “Solomon states…” or “The campaign describes…” |
| D — testimony pending proof | Family account, oral history or internal project document without independent corroboration | Private use only, labelled “verification pending” |

No claim moves from C or D to A merely because it is repeated on several ST-Firm-controlled websites or documents.

## 4. Kamnarok finding

### 4.1 What the public record supports

The public evidence confirms a serious and long-running land dispute involving communities and farmers around Lake Kamnarok:

- The National Land Commission matter is identified as **Kamnarok Farmers Group v Kenya Wildlife Service and 2 others**, reference **NLC/HLI/153/2017**.
- The published determination records the Commission's treatment of the compulsory acquisition as a historical land injustice and includes remedial recommendations.
- On **23 May 2024**, the Environment and Land Court at Iten issued an interim conservatory order restraining further evictions, burning, demolition and crop destruction pending further proceedings.
- The court material identifies the petitioners through recognised officials of the Kamnarok Farmers Group.
- Public reporting and county records locate the dispute around Lake Kamnarok in Barwessa Ward, Baringo North, and record community demands concerning land, boundaries, compensation and participation.

### 4.2 What the public record does not yet support

The NLC determination, the 2024 court order and the public reports reviewed do **not** name Solomon Saigut Cherogony as a claimant, petitioner, advocate, task-force member or organiser.

Therefore the statement that he was **“among the brains who helped the farmers take back or protect the land”** is currently:

- **Grade:** D — important family testimony, verification pending.
- **Private wording:** “The family records Solomon as one of the strategic minds who assisted the Kamnarok farmers' effort; documentary and witness verification is in progress.”
- **Public wording now:** Do not make the personal attribution yet.

This is not a finding that Solomon had no role. It is a finding that the reviewed public record does not prove the role.

### 4.3 Evidence needed to pass the Kamnarok gate

Collect and authenticate:

1. Kamnarok Farmers Group or task-force meeting minutes naming Solomon.
2. Appointment, delegation, attendance or working-group records.
3. Letters, memoranda, petitions or briefing papers authored, signed or transmitted by him.
4. Affidavits or witness statements from recognised group officials describing his specific contribution and dates.
5. Dated photographs, recordings or media coverage with reliable captions.
6. KENAFF correspondence, if his involvement arose through a KENAFF role.
7. Consent from the relevant group and document owners before campaign publication.

The final claim must describe what he actually did—such as convening, strategy, evidence preparation, farmer coordination, funding professional help or institutional liaison—rather than using a broad heroic phrase that cannot be tested.
