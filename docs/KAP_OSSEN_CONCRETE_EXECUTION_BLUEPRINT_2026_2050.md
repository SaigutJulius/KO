## 1. The concrete decision

Kap Ossen will become an intergenerational family-development institution with one heritage foundation and several strictly separated delivery lanes.

It will not be one overloaded website, one informal family committee, one political campaign or one speculative city promise.

The programme has six connected outcomes:

1. **Preserve the family story** through a governed Family Embassy and archive.
2. **Create opportunity** through education, agriculture, technology, enterprise and diaspora knowledge.
3. **Establish accountable governance** through the Family Charter, Family Assembly, proposed Patron and Family Development Council.
4. **Actualise ST-Firm** as a credible, protected and commercially sustainable technology institution operating from Berlin, Deutschland and, when justified, Kenya.
5. **Test the proposed 30-acre Gateway** as a phased, productive family-development demonstrator after land and feasibility gates.
6. **Develop ARROR City Legacy toward 2050** as Engineer Saigut Julius Kipkorir's evidence-led long-range vision, never as an unsupported municipal, territorial or investment claim.

The 2027 Solomon Ops mission and the 31 October 2029 SCOF target review support parts of the story but do not control the family's permanent purpose.
