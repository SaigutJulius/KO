# Kap Ossen Family x ST-Firm Development Compact

## Solomon Ops Actualisation Mission: 1 August 2026 to 31 July 2027

**Document type:** Private planning brief  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared for:** Engineer Saigut Julius Kipkorir, the Kap Ossen family and authorised ST-Firm leadership  
**Status:** Draft for family, corporate and legal review; not approved for public release

## 1. Mission statement

From **1 August 2026 through 31 July 2027**, the partnership's primary development mission is to help actualise **ST-Firm** and to build and operate **Solomon Ops as the lawful campaign-operations programme for Hon. Solomon Saigut Cherogony's 2027 Eldama Ravine parliamentary bid**.

This is a defined one-year execution mandate. It does not replace the permanent purpose, independence or heritage of the Kap Ossen family. Participation is voluntary, contribution does not automatically create company ownership, and family relationships must never be used to pressure anyone into providing money, labour, data or political support.

The mission has four coordinated but legally and operationally distinct tracks:

1. ST-Firm corporate actualisation and protection of its reusable technology.
2. Solomon Ops 2027 campaign-operations actualisation.
3. A private Kap Ossen family presentation and voluntary-support compact.
4. UDA nomination and general-election readiness for Hon. Solomon Saigut Cherogony.

## 2. Strategic outcome by 31 July 2027

By the end of the mission period:

- ST-Firm should be a properly governed, documented and commercially credible technology company.
- Solomon Ops should be a secure, demonstrable and auditable campaign-operations environment with defined users, workflows, controls and evidence of value.
- Kap Ossen family members who choose to participate should have clear roles based on skill, experience and availability.
- Any work connected to Hon. Solomon Saigut Cherogony's UDA nomination should be compliant, voluntary and completely separated from ST-Firm customers, KENAFF members, SCOF participants and private family records.
- The family should possess a written decision register, contribution register, risk register and handover pack that survives beyond one person or one event.

## 3. The four-track partnership model

### Track A — ST-Firm corporate actualisation

**Purpose:** Make ST-Firm institutionally ready to build, contract, invoice, protect its intellectual property and serve customers.

**Core work:**

- Confirm company governance, authorised decision-makers and signing authority.
- Establish a corporate records room for registration, tax, contracts, policies and intellectual property.
- Define the ST-Firm product portfolio and a single commercial narrative.
- Establish Kenya and Deutschland operating responsibilities without implying registrations or licences that do not exist.
- Produce customer proposals, service agreements, pricing, support terms and a delivery method.
- Build a credible demonstration environment and select two or three suitable pilot partners.
- Create finance controls, a monthly management report and a conflict-of-interest register.
- Protect the ST-Firm name, designs, code, documentation and confidential methods.

**Success evidence:** Governance pack approved; product catalogue published internally; contracts ready; pilot pipeline documented; first paid or formally approved pilot secured; monthly reporting active.

### Track B — Solomon Ops 2027 campaign operations

**Purpose:** Build Solomon Ops as ST-Firm's purpose-specific, lawfully governed operations programme for Hon. Solomon Saigut Cherogony's 2027 Eldama Ravine MP bid. It is not a neutral KENAFF or family platform and it is not an umbrella for every ST-Firm activity.

**Minimum viable product:**

- Secure sign-in and role-based access.
- Mission and project dashboard.
- Task ownership, deadlines, dependencies and escalation.
- Contact and stakeholder records with purpose-based access.
- Meetings, decisions and evidence register.
- Opportunity and risk radar.
- Document control with version history.
- Activity audit trail and incident reporting.
- Mobile-friendly field updates.
- Exportable management reports.

**Product boundaries:**

- Solomon Ops must not become a covert voter-profiling, persuasion or surveillance system.
- Political opinions, party membership, family details and location data require strict legal review and enhanced protection.
- KENAFF farmer information, SCOF user or treasury information, ST-Firm customer information and Kap Ossen private records cannot be copied into a political workspace.
- ST-Firm must give the reusable non-political technology a neutral product name, provision it through separate tenants and contracts, and prevent the Solomon Ops campaign brand from appearing in KENAFF or other non-political institutional environments.
- Campaign staff, volunteers, accounts, devices, domains, communications and data stores must be separately authorised and ring-fenced.

**Success evidence:** Product specification approved; security model tested; core workflows demonstrated; pilot report completed; audit logging covers every sensitive action; no critical unresolved privacy or security finding.

### Track C — Kap Ossen family capability partnership

**Purpose:** Present a disciplined, evidence-led case for why family members may choose to support Solomon's 2027 bid, while protecting each person's right to decline political participation.

**Contribution lanes:**

- Governance and elder guidance.
- Engineering, construction and land-development insight.
- Agriculture and producer-market knowledge.
- Finance, accounting and controls.
- Education, mentorship and youth participation.
- Diaspora market and professional connections.
- Story, archive and approved family-history documentation.
- Product testing, introductions and pilot feedback.
- Lawful campaign logistics, professional advice and community listening where a participant expressly chooses a political role.

**Participation rules:**

- Every participant chooses a written role, time commitment and end date.
- Cash, equipment, professional time and introductions are recorded separately.
- A contribution is not an automatic donation, loan, investment, shareholding or political contribution; its legal character must be written before acceptance.
- Family members retain the right to decline, withdraw and control the publication of their biography or photograph.
- Private family discussions remain outside the public website unless expressly approved.

**Success evidence:** Skills register completed; workstream owners appointed; monthly family mission briefing held; contributions recorded; disputes and conflicts documented and resolved through the agreed forum.

### Track D — nomination and election-readiness track

**Purpose:** Prepare Hon. Solomon Saigut Cherogony for lawful participation in the applicable UDA nomination process and, if nominated, the 2027 general election. Solomon Ops is the campaign operations instrument; the Kap Ossen heritage platform, ST-Firm corporate records, KENAFF and SCOF remain separate and must not be converted into campaign databases or implied endorsements.

**Date discipline:** The currently published IEBC Election Operation Plan places party-primary activity between **17 March and 10 April 2027**, the resolution of intra-party disputes through **9 May 2027**, and submission of successful party nominees between **10 and 20 May 2027**. UDA must still issue its applicable party notice and requirements. Therefore, June–July 2027 is **not** planned as the UDA primary window; it is the post-primary election-readiness, corporate-continuity and mission-handover period. The live plan must follow later binding UDA, IEBC and legal notices if the timetable changes.

**Permitted readiness work:**

- Maintain an eligibility and documentary-requirements checklist.
- Track official UDA and IEBC notices.
- Appoint a qualified Kenyan election-law and data-protection adviser.
- Establish a separately named team, account, budget, data store, devices and communications channels.
- Record volunteer consent, authorised responsibilities and expenditure evidence.
- Prepare an issues-and-development record using public or consented information.
- Train the authorised team on nomination rules, electoral conduct and data protection.
- Conduct a pre-submission documentation and compliance audit.

**Prohibited mixing:**

- No KENAFF farmer, chapter or programme database may be used for nomination support.
- No SCOF wallet, user, transaction, treasury or community data may be used for nomination support.
- No ST-Firm client list, employee record or product telemetry may be repurposed for political activity.
- No private Kap Ossen family directory may be treated as a political-support list.
- No access to services, opportunities, jobs, loans, land benefits or technology may depend on political support.
- No public resources, organisational authority or implied institutional endorsement may be used without lawful written authority.
- No political publication goes onto the Kap Ossen heritage site or ST-Firm corporate site by default. Solomon Ops campaign communications must use their own approved identity, domain, privacy notice and publication authority.

**Success evidence:** Official requirements tracker current; separate governance and finance operational; all volunteers documented; privacy impact assessment completed where necessary; pre-nomination compliance sign-off obtained.

## 4. The private family case for support

ST-Firm's presentation to the Kap Ossen family should make a clear proposition: **support is invited because there is a serious candidate, a governed operating plan and a long-range development vision—not merely because Solomon is a family member.**

The presentation should follow this order:

1. Kap Ossen heritage, Guga's sacrifice and the family's duty to future generations.
2. Hon. Solomon's verified public-service, business and community record.
3. Engineer Saigut Julius Kipkorir's ARROR City Legacy and 30-acre Gateway vision.
4. ST-Firm's execution capability and the Solomon Ops campaign-control system.
5. Solomon's 2027–2032 constituency agenda, expressed as costed and legally deliverable priorities.
6. Voluntary contribution lanes for family members: advice, verification, professional skill, time, lawful finance or logistics.
7. Safeguards covering consent, privacy, conflicts, accounts, publication and withdrawal.

ARROR City Legacy remains Julius's private originating vision. Its inclusion in the family presentation does not transfer it to Solomon, the campaign or the family collectively. Any public campaign use requires Julius's written permission and factual, legal and land-authority review.

### 4.1 Evidence standard for Solomon's record

Use three labels throughout the presentation:

- **Verified public record** — supported by an official or independently reliable source and matched to the correct person.
- **Candidate or family account** — stated by Solomon, the family or an internal project document but not independently verified.
- **Pending evidence** — potentially important but excluded from public claims until records or credible witnesses are obtained.

As of 1 August 2026, the strongest official trace found is a Kenya Gazette entry naming **Solomon Cherogony** as an Eldama Ravine Sub-County Hospital board member representing the business community. Because the Gazette omits his middle names, the identity match must still be confirmed before publication.

The claim that Solomon was “among the brains” behind the Kamnarok farmers' land-recovery effort is preserved as **family testimony pending evidence**. The published National Land Commission determination and the 2024 court material confirm the farmers' dispute and protective legal action, but the public documents reviewed do not name Solomon. Obtain meeting minutes, appointment letters, correspondence, affidavits, photographs and statements from recognised Kamnarok Farmers Group or task-force officials before making the attribution publicly.

## 5. Governance and accountability

### 5.1 Partnership council

Create a **Kap Ossen–ST-Firm Development Council** for this mission period.

Suggested seats:

- Mission sponsor and ST-Firm founder: Engineer Saigut Julius Kipkorir.
- Proposed Patron and family-development convener: Hon. Solomon Saigut Cherogony, subject to Family Assembly appointment, his written acceptance and the Patron mandate.
- Family heritage representative.
- ST-Firm product/engineering lead.
- Finance and controls lead.
- Legal, election-compliance and data-protection adviser.
- Programme secretary responsible for decisions, actions and evidence.

The council coordinates the compact; it does not acquire ownership of ST-Firm, family land or any member's personal work merely by existing.

The proposed Patron is the mission guardian, convener and approved family-development ambassador. The office may call meetings, request reports, refer material risks and present the annual Legacy Address. It does not, acting alone, transfer land, commit family funds, bind ST-Firm, license ARROR City Legacy, access private records or approve political activity in the Kap Ossen name. Solomon must recuse from Kap Ossen decisions concerning Solomon Ops, his campaign, his personal business or another direct interest.

The long-term constitutional model, Patron term and succession, standing committees, decision-rights matrix and five-capitals sustainability system are specified in `KAP_OSSEN_GOVERNANCE_SUSTAINABILITY_PATRON_PLAN.md`.

### 5.2 Decision rights

### 5.3 Core controls

- One monthly council meeting with minutes, decisions, owners and deadlines.
- One programme dashboard showing green, amber and red status.
- One conflict-of-interest register reviewed monthly.
- Separate budgets and bank/payment channels for family development, ST-Firm business and political activity.
- Separate data inventories, access lists and retention rules.
- Two-person approval for material spending or publication.
- Quarterly legal, privacy and information-security review.

## 8. Finance and ownership rules

Maintain five visibly separated financial lanes. The first three are mandatory during the Year-One compact; the fourth and fifth apply whenever SCOF or a Gateway project vehicle is active:

1. **ST-Firm business ledger** — investment, revenue, costs, contracts and ownership instruments.
2. **Kap Ossen family-development ledger** — approved family project costs and documented voluntary contributions.
3. **Political-activity ledger** — only lawful, properly documented nomination or campaign-related receipts and expenditure under professional advice.
4. **SCOF product/ecosystem ledger** — issuer, treasury, product, compliance and operating activity according to its final legal structure.
5. **Gateway/ARROR project-vehicle ledger** — land, development, tenant, financing and operating activity only after lawful formation.

Before accepting any material contribution, issue a short instrument stating whether it is a gift, reimbursable expense, loan, investment, paid service or in-kind political contribution. Never promise equity, land rights, employment, procurement or preferential access informally.

## 13. Source and timing note

This plan deliberately avoids presenting June or July 2027 as an official UDA nomination date. Kenya's Constitution provides that general elections are held on the second Tuesday in August every fifth year. The Elections Act requires political parties to nominate candidates within the statutory election timetable, while UDA's own constitution and nomination rules govern its method and eligibility. The currently published IEBC Election Operation Plan places party primaries between 17 March and 10 April 2027, but exact UDA dates and requirements remain subject to the party's applicable notice and any later binding change.
