## 11. ST-Firm incorporation and corporate foundation

No website should say “ST-Firm GmbH,” “ST-Firm UG,” “ST-Firm Limited” or “registered company” until official records support that exact wording.

### 11.1 Stage 0 — legal-status audit

Collect and verify:

- current legal or business-name registration, if any;
- founder/owner identity and address records;
- Berlin trade registration status;
- tax registration and ELSTER status;
- existing contracts, invoices, bank/payment accounts and liabilities;
- beneficial ownership and contributors;
- source-code authorship and contractor rights;
- current domains, hosting accounts, repositories and administrator access;
- Kenyan activities, workers, contracts, permanent-establishment and tax exposure;
- any existing ST-Firm, SCOF, Solomon Ops, ARROR City or GREEN-KI SSOS filings.

### 11.2 Deutschland structure decision

| Option | Suitable when | Main planning concern |
|---|---|---|
| Sole trader/Gewerbe | Early validated services with simple ownership | Personal liability and weaker separation of IP/business risk |
| UG (haftungsbeschränkt) | Limited-liability start with modest capital | Must be adequately capitalised; reserve and formal compliance obligations |
| GmbH | Stronger long-term corporate platform and investor/client credibility | Higher capital and formal setup burden |

Recommended direction for professional review: establish a properly capitalised limited-liability parent or IP/technology entity in Deutschland—UG if the business is still capital-constrained, with a planned path to GmbH; GmbH when capital and operations justify it. Do not use the legal minimum as the operating budget.

Official guidance in Deutschland states that GmbH/UG formation requires notarised articles and Commercial Register entry; Berlin trade activity also requires the applicable Gewerbeanmeldung. Tax onboarding must be completed through the proper process.

### 11.3 Kenya structure decision

Evaluate with Kenyan corporate and tax advisers:

1. Kenyan private limited operating subsidiary.
2. Registered branch of the entity established in Deutschland.
3. Independent Kenyan services company under a written intercompany/licence arrangement.

The preferred long-term architecture is usually a clear technology/IP entity in Deutschland plus a properly governed Kenyan operating entity when local contracting, employment, campaign delivery, agricultural services or data processing becomes material. Final choice depends on residence, management, tax, banking, procurement and regulatory advice.
