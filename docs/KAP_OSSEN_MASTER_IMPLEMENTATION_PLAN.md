# Kap Ossen Digital Family Embassy and 30-Acre Vision

## Detailed Master Implementation Plan

**Plan version:** 1.0

**Status:** Detailed planning baseline; implementation begins only after approval  
**Primary sponsor:** Engineer Saigut Julius Kipkorir  
**Project:** Kap Ossen Family Development Platform

## 1. Executive vision

Build a future-facing digital family institution that does four jobs exceptionally well:

1. preserves the history and identity of the descendants of the late Dickson Ossen Cherogony;
2. connects family members across Kenya and the diaspora;
3. presents the proposed 30-acre Eldama Ravine development professionally;
4. provides a secure operational environment for family decisions, opportunities and projects.

The product will combine an elegant public **Family Embassy** with a protected **Family Situation Room**. The design will feel ceremonial, technologically advanced and internationally credible while remaining grounded in ARROR, Baringo, agriculture, family service and stewardship.

## 2. Project principles

### 2.1 Family first

Technology supports family relationships and responsibility. It does not replace personal discussion or elder authority.

### 2.2 Public truth, private dignity

Public pages contain only confirmed facts and approved media. Sensitive family, will, land, contact and financial information remains private.

### 2.3 Proposals are labelled as proposals

The 30-acre mall, market, park and logistics concepts must not be presented as approved construction until title, authority, feasibility, planning and environmental gates are completed.

### 2.4 Original identity

Apple, Lidl, Amazon, KENAFF and ST-Firm may provide experience, operational or partnership references, but Kap Ossen receives an original name, visual identity and architecture.

### 2.5 Accessibility and performance

The site must work on an affordable phone and a high-end desktop. Motion must never block access, navigation or understanding.

### 2.6 Measurable progress

Every major initiative should have an owner, status, evidence, next action and review date.

## 14. Security and privacy

Minimum controls:

- deny private access by default;
- separate public and private data;
- role checks on every restricted operation;
- no private document URLs exposed publicly;
- consent tracking;
- audit events for uploads, edits and permission changes;
- secure administrator accounts;
- session expiry;
- backup and recovery plan;
- incident-response contact;
- removal process for withdrawn consent.

## 15. Accessibility requirements

- semantic headings and landmarks;
- keyboard-complete navigation;
- visible focus states;
- sufficient colour contrast;
- 44-pixel touch targets;
- descriptive alternative text;
- captions/transcripts for audio and video;
- reduced-motion mode;
- no information communicated only through colour;
- form labels and understandable errors;
- responsive presentation without horizontal scrolling.

## 16. Performance requirements

- fast first viewport on mobile networks;
- compressed and responsive images;
- limited animation/particle work;
- no unnecessary third-party scripts;
- pause animation outside the viewport or hidden tab;
- stable page layout;
- route-level loading states where needed;
- production build must succeed without warnings that affect users.
